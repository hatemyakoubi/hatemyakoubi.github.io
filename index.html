About

I lead architecture and full-stack development for Unicore, a plugin-based warehouse management platform built on ASP.NET Core, NHibernate, and TypeScript. Most of my work is migrating legacy .NET Framework modules into the current architecture incrementally, and designing the integration layers that let independent modules work together without tight coupling.

Alongside that, I focus on where AI genuinely helps a development team — I built an AI-assisted visual quality control feature using image comparison, and I work with my team on integrating AI coding assistants into daily workflows.

Before this, I spent five years as a programming instructor, and I still mentor junior engineers and students on their final-year projects today. I speak Arabic natively, French fluently, and English professionally.
